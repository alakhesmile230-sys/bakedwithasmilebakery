About Us

Baked with Love, Served with a Smile

Welcome to Baked with a Smile Bakery! We’re a family-owned bakery based right here in Cape Town, and we’ve been spreading sweetness since 2024.

It all started in a small home kitchen. We wanted to create baked goods that taste like home - warm, fresh, and made with love. What began as baking for friends and family quickly grew into a bakery that our community loves.

Our Mission

To bake happiness into every bite. We believe that good food brings people together, whether it’s a birthday cake, morning bread, or just a cookie with coffee.

What Makes Us Special?

  • Fresh Daily: Everything is baked fresh every morning. No preservatives, no shortcuts.
  • Custom Orders: From weddings to birthdays, we create cakes that match your theme and taste.
  • Local Ingredients: We support local farmers and use the best Cape Town ingredients.
  • Catering: Need treats for an event? We’ve got you covered with platters and dessert tables.

Meet the Baker

Hi! I’m Alakhe Smile, the head baker and founder. Baking has been my passion since I was a kid watching my gogo in the kitchen. Today, I get to share that love with you, one loaf and one cake at a time.
