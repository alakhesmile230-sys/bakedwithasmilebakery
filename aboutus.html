Baked with a Smile Bakery

We are a neighbourhood bakery in Cape Town that believes in one simple thing: fresh ingredients, traditional methods, and service with a smile.


What started as a love for baking at home has grown into a local favourite for cakes, bread, pastries and sweet treats for every occasion.


While our customers love visiting our physical store, we are now expanding online to make it easier for you to explore our products, find information, and get in touch - anytime, anywhere.

Our Mission

To provide freshly baked, high-quality products that bring happiness to every customer while delivering exceptional service and creating memorable experiences for every occasion.

Our Vision

To become one of South Africa's most recognised neighbourhood bakeries by combining traditional baking with modern technology and outstanding customer service.
